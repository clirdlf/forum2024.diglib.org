Hotel Accommodations

Team DLF is pleased to offer small room blocks at a handful of hotels near the Michigan State University campus from Sunday, July 28 through Thursday, August 1. We encourage participants to book early as blocks will likely fill up quickly. 

Note that the numbers below indicate the total rooms available and may already be sold out. Last day to book rooms at any location is July 1. 

If you need a feather- or fragrance-free room, or any other accessibility accommodations, please communicate those directly to the hotel when booking.

Looking for more information about the conference venue, emergency information, accessibility, or transportation? Find this and much more on the Resources for Attendees page.

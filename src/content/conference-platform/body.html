About Zoom events

The Virtual DLF Forum will take place on Zoom Events. Zoom Events provides a hub for the event, which allows registered attendees to connect with one another through the Lobby and direct messaging (if opted-in) and to easily view the program schedule, speakers, and sponsors. To join Zoom Events that you are registered for, you can click the join link in the confirmation email, calendar entry, or the ticket in your Zoom Events account. You can also join Zoom Events from the Zoom desktop client, a web browser, Zoom mobile app, or the events detail page. 

Important: You must be logged in to Zoom with the email address you used to register for the event. 

Days before the event, we recommend updating your Zoom Client for the best possible experience. See this Zoom support article: Updating Zoom to the latest version. See also: Joining Zoom Events (Zoom support article).

Attendees will register through Conftool and will be added to Zoom Events by Team DLF. 

To join the virtual event, please login to Zoom using the email you provided during registration

Not sure which email you registered with? Please email us at forum@diglib.org and/or DLFforum@concentra-cms.com.

What to expect

About the Sessions

There are 34 sessions led by 102 speakers and one Featured Speaker presentation (during the opening plenary) happening over a period of two days: October 22-23. There are also three activities scheduled during the two breaks (one per day). 

With exception of the opening plenary and lightning talks, during session times, there will be four sessions held simultaneously.

Recordings

Sessions will be recorded and made available to registered attendees only. The opening plenary will be made available publicly on the CLIR/DLF YouTube account after the event. 

Captions

All sessions will have captioning available. The opening plenary will feature a live captioner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to Register for the Event?

Yes, you should have already completed your registration through the ConfTool Registration Platform. You will NOT register for the event in the Zoom Platform. Once you are added to the Zoom Events platform, you will receive an email from Zoom regarding how to join. 

Registration was open from July 9 through October 15. 

What kind of technology should I be using to join the Zoom sessions?

For the best experience, please join by desktop and use an external microphone, for example a headset with a microphone. We highly recommend updating to the latest Zoom client/mobile application.

Where is the Zoom link?

Zoom Events automatically sends you a link to join the event when we add you to our system. Look for an email from noreply-zoomevents@zoom.edu. It may have gone to spam.

If your organization uses email aliases, please get in touch with us at DLFforum@concentra-cms.com.

Registration Cancellation Policy

Refunds for registration are available until Tuesday, October 1, 2024. There are no refunds after this date. A $15 processing fee for cancellation will be charged for all refunds. All cancellations and requests for refunds must be made in writing by October 1, 2024. Please email your request to forum@diglib.org.
